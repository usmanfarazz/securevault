Privacy Policy — SecureVault

Last updated: 23 September 2026

SecureVault is an offline password manager and password strength checker. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: SecureVault does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

1. What data we collect

None. SecureVault has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. We do not collect your name, email, device identifiers, usage data, or any other information.

2. Your passwords and vault data

Any passwords, usernames or notes you save are stored only on your own device, inside your browser's local storage. They are encrypted using AES-256-GCM, with the encryption key derived from your master password via PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256. Your master password and your saved data are never sent over the internet and are never transmitted to the developer or any third party.

3. No third-party services

The app makes no network requests for its core features. It contains no advertising, no trackers and no third-party SDKs.

4. Permissions

SecureVault does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files. It may use your device's clipboard only when you tap "Copy", and it clears the clipboard shortly afterwards.

5. Data deletion

Because your data never leaves your device, you are always in full control. You can delete individual entries in the app, or clear all data by clearing the app's storage / uninstalling the app. There is no account to delete because no account is ever created.

6. Children's privacy

SecureVault does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page.
